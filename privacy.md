# Privacy Policy for Blast

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

Blast respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled when you use the game.

## Information we collect

Blast may collect and store:

- Your username and internal account identifier
- Your selected profile avatar
- Campaign progress, scores, wins, and gameplay statistics
- Daily challenge and leaderboard results
- Custom levels, level names, likes, and download activity
- Account creation and progress timestamps

Blast does not intentionally collect your real name, physical address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, videos, audio, or financial information.

## How information is used

This information is used to:

- Create and manage your account
- Save and synchronize game progress
- Display leaderboards and player profiles
- provide community-level features
- Maintain and improve game functionality
- Prevent misuse of game services

## Public information

Your username, selected avatar, leaderboard results, published custom levels, level names, and related statistics may be visible to other Blast players.

## Data storage

Blast uses Supabase as a service provider for authentication and database storage. Information transmitted between the game and Supabase is protected using encrypted network connections.

We do not sell users' personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

Information is retained while your account remains active or as necessary to operate the game. You may request deletion of your account and associated data through our [Account Deletion page](delete-account.html).

When an account is deleted, associated profile information, game progress, statistics, likes, and custom-level information will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required for security or legal reasons.

## Children's privacy

Blast is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
