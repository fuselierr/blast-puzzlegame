# Delete Your Blast Account

This page allows players to request deletion of a **Blast** account and its associated data.

## Request deletion

To request account deletion:

1. Open the deletion-request form below.
2. Provide your Blast username and a contact email.
3. Do not provide your password.
4. Submit the request.
5. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account.

[Submit an account deletion request](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScm9OXtO9l3cJgg25IjCCYG-LwHEdg-mEB7eWKZWI64uccCYA/viewform?usp=dialog)

## Data that will be deleted

After account ownership is verified, the following data associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized:

- Account and profile information
- Selected avatar
- Campaign progress
- Scores and gameplay statistics
- Daily challenge results
- Likes and other account activity
- Unpublished custom levels

Published community levels may be deleted or anonymized by removing their association with the account.

## Processing time

Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days. We will notify you using the contact email supplied in the request.

Some information may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
